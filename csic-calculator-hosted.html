Lifetime Spending Power Calculator

Works out the level annual spending a client can sustain in today's dollars, drawing on registered and non-registered assets, pension and government benefits, with the accounts running down to zero at the chosen life expectancy.

Government Benefits

Ask the client whether they will qualify for the full CPP retirement pension. Full CPP requires contributions at or near the maximum for roughly 39 years, and most clients receive considerably less.
